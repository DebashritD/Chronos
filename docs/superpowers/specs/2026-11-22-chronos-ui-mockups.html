Chronos: UI v2 (native app feel, dark)

Tweak: each Today card shows how long is left to mark it, counted to midnight in your timezone at the end of the current period. Daily habits show hours (e.g. "14h left"); every-N-days and weekly habits show days and hours (e.g. "1d 14h left"). The chip turns green once marked, and orange in the last 3 hours (see "Stretch" on desktop). Sample data, with today as Wed 18 Nov. The home screen becomes Today: one tap marks each habit Green, Yellow or Red. Tap the habit name to open the sheet with a note. Week and Month move to a Progress tab. Colours use the iOS system palette, the font is Inter, and the cards are rounded with soft depth.
